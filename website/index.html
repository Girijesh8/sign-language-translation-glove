ABOUT THE PROJECT

Why ISL Bridge?

Understanding the problem behind the technology.

🤝

The Communication Gap

Communication can become difficult when people who do not know Indian Sign Language interact with Deaf or hard-of-hearing individuals.

🧤

Our Approach

Our prototype uses a sensor-equipped glove to capture predefined finger-bending patterns and translate them into digital information.

🔊

Accessible Output

Recognized output can be transmitted wirelessly and presented as text or converted into speech.
